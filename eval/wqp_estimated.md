## What one below-limit flag would hide

- **1,465 of 27,444 below-limit results (5.3 %) were detections**, not non-detections; for the trace metals alone 996 of 1,900 (52.4 %). WISE-6 records all of them with the same flag.
- **706 estimated results carry a detection limit and no quantification limit.** Collapsed into a censored result against the only limit reported, each becomes [0, LOD], an interval that excludes every concentration the laboratory reported finding.
- 759 carry a quantification or reporting limit only, and are written as [0, LOQ] with the detection recorded by the class.
- **0 carry both**, so the closed interval [LOD, LOQ] is never supplied by this record.
- 63 quantified results report a value below a quantification or reporting limit attached to them — the self-contradicting row the Waterbase assessment types `BoundNotEstablished`.

Reported by: 21NEV1_WQX 654, USGS-CA 473, SIR_WQX 260, BVR 71, USGS-AZ 7. Most frequent analytes: Silver 71, Cadmium 70, Beryllium 68, Chromium 68, Molybdenum 68, Lead 67, Cobalt 66, Thallium 65.
