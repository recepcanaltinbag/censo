## Provenance

- PubChem PUG REST, `classification_type=simple`
- Use categories accepted only from: Consumer Product Information Database (CPID), EPA Chemical and Products Database (CPDat), Medical Subject Headings (MeSH)
- ChEBI used for ontology alignment (`skos:exactMatch`), not for use
- Raw responses cached under `derived/interim/pubchem_cache/` so the resolution is reproducible without network access
