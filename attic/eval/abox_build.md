## What is deliberately not asserted

No compliance verdict appears in this graph. Verdicts are materialised by the rule layer and SHACL, so the published knowledge graph does not prejudge an assessment that depends on which regulation is applied.

Every reported zero is kept verbatim in `censo:reportedValue`, and every reconstructed status carries `censo:censoringRecovered true`, so a consumer can distinguish what was measured from what was inferred.
