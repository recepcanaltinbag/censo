Generated by `scripts/24_dual_regulation.py`. The observations, the unit conversion and the decision procedure are identical in both columns; only the regulation package differs -- including the rule it states for a censored result, the band it reads a measurement with, and the conditions it attaches to its thresholds, all read from the package file.

 - **EU**: censored-result rule `point`, U = 0.5 T, 3 conditional analyte(s)
 - **TR**: censored-result rule `point`, U = 0.5 T, 0 conditional analyte(s) — the package states no condition and no rule of its own, so the vocabulary's default is applied and said so here

## Where they disagree, and why

Two distinct effects, reported separately because a single figure would let the larger one hide the more interesting one.

**Coverage.** Over all 1,488,677 scored assessments, 1,050,622 (70.6%, 95% CI 70.5–70.6%) change outcome. Most of that is one jurisdiction regulating a substance the other does not, which is a fact about the two legal instruments rather than about the measurements.

**Substance.** Restricting to the 325,011 assessments both jurisdictions regulate, **51,902 (16.0%, 95% CI 15.8–16.1%) still change outcome.** Here nothing differs but the numeric standard, so this is the figure that tests whether the threshold has to be swappable.

The two rows worth reading twice are the ones that cross the compliance boundary: an assessment that is *Exceeding* under one instrument and *Compliant* under the other, and one the EU sets aside under Article 3(3b) as method-insufficient while Türkiye's looser standard lets the same method decide it. Neither is expressible if the threshold is a column.

## What this does and does not establish

Turkish standards are not in force over European rivers, so the TR column is counterfactual. It is reported to show that the assessment machinery is regulation-independent: neither the ontology, the rule layer nor this script contains a threshold. Both columns are produced by the same code reading two published files.

- **EU** — Directive 2008/105/EC, Annex I; 56 annual-average standards.
- **TR** — YSKY 2016, Table 4; 217 annual-average standards.
