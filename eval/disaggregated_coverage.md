# How far the disaggregated release reaches

Generated by `scripts/27b_disaggregated_coverage.py`.

A maximum allowable concentration applies to an individual measurement, so assessing it needs sample-level data. Sample-level reporting is not uniform between Member States, so every maximum-allowable result in this paper describes the stratum below and not the record.

| | |
|---|---|
| river station-years in the aggregated release | **4,170,005** |
| river samples in the disaggregated release | **55,358,021** |
| of those station-years reached by at least one sample | **1,234,302** (29.6 %) |
| samples per covered station-year, median | **6** (quartiles 4–12) |

The median is reported rather than the mean because the distribution is skewed: a few station-years carry hundreds of samples and most carry a handful, so a mean would describe neither.
