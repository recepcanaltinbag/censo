# Is the between-country spread an artefact?

Generated by `scripts/25_country_confounders.py`. The quantity is the share of station-years carrying neither a censoring flag nor a quantification limit — a WISE-6 schema field, identical for every reporter, and not a national threshold.

- countries above the 5,000 station-year floor: **26**
- common basket: **76** substances reported by at least 60% of them

## The spread under four treatments

| Treatment | Range | Countries |

| Raw, as plotted | 3.3–97.4% | 26 |

| Common basket only | 12.9–97.3% | 26 |
| EU Member States, standardised | 8.9–97.3% | 22 |

The fourth treatment we intended -- restricting to 2015 onwards -- is **not reported as a robustness test**, because this release does not support one. Only 6 of the countries here have any river record dated 2015 or later; France, Italy, Finland, Poland, Austria and Hungary have none at all. The per-country column is kept in the CSV because it is a real property of the release, but a range computed over 6 countries, one of which supplies most of the rows, would not test anything. It is instead reported as a change over time, in `eval/waterbase_external.md`, where the whole audit is split at 2015: 2015+ is nearly half the record, but it comes from these few reporters, so it describes their practice and not Europe's.

Kendall's tau between the raw and standardised orderings is **+0.89**, so standardisation does not reorder the countries; it leaves the same ones at each end.

## Reading

Standardisation re-weights every country onto the same substance basket, so a country cannot appear tidy merely because it monitors substances that are usually reported with a limit. The common-basket and recent-window columns remove the national speciality determinands and the oldest returns respectively. The last row drops every reporter for which Annex I is not law.

Restricting to EU Member States matters for a second reason: the quantification-limit criterion of Directive 2009/90/EC binds them and not the other reporters, so only their column is a compliance statement rather than a description.
