## What a two-valued pipeline returns for the same rows

Counted row by row, not assumed, and under all three substitution conventions in routine use: a non-detection entering the calculation at zero, at half the quantification limit, or at the limit itself. Reporting only one would make the comparison an artefact of our own choice of rule.

A record with no usable number at all is read as zero under every rule, which is the silent substitution this paper is about.
