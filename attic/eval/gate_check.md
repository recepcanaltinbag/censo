## Q1 — How large is the 'zero-substituted non-detect' problem?

- Chemical measurements (conventional + metal + micropollutant): **75,675**

| determinand group | measurements | stored as 0.0 | share |

| Conventional | 3,900 | 345 | 8.8% |

- Recorded as exactly `0.0`: **58,176 (76.9%)**
- Micropollutants alone: 56,768 / 66,600 (**85.2%**)

A reported `0.0` is not a measurement of zero concentration; it is a non-detect with its censoring information erased. Every load, mean and sum computed from this table is biased low by an unknown amount (Helsel 2006).
