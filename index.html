What it is

CENSO extends SOSA/SSN for measurements produced by a method that has a limit of detection. Its single modelling commitment is that the detection limit belongs to the analytical run, not to the instrument. Because the limit travels with the result, a non-detection can be typed as the interval [0, LOD] it actually establishes rather than stored as a zero, and compliance becomes four-valued: compliant, exceeding, possibly exceeding, and cannot be determined.

The fourth outcome is not a convenience:

Where a result is referred to as “less than limit of quantification”, and the limit of quantification of that technique is above the EQS, the result for the substance being measured shall not be considered for the purposes of assessing the overall chemical status of that water body.
— Directive 2008/105/EC, Article 3(3b)

That outcome is neither compliant nor exceeding. A two-valued data model cannot express it, so compliance with the provision cannot be audited.

Reuse

Built on SOSA/SSN, PROV-O, SKOS and QUDT. Every threshold carries cereg:transcriptionStatus cereg:VerifiedAgainstPrimarySource, because both packages are parsed from the legal texts themselves rather than from a secondary compilation.
