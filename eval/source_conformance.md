## Reading the violation

**A non-detect carrying a positive number** is an assertion the reporter made: the record says the concentration is below the quantification limit and simultaneously states a value for it. Substitution has already been applied at source. Where the flag survives this is visible and reversible; where it does not, it is neither.

An earlier version of the shapes required every method to state a limit of *detection*, and so fired on every row: WISE-6 has no field for one, and both provisions at issue --- Article 3(3b) of Directive 2008/105/EC and Article 4(1) of Directive 2009/90/EC --- are written about the quantification limit. This pipeline satisfied that constraint by manufacturing the datum at LOQ/3, which is why the constraint was wrong and the manufactured value is gone: the shapes now require *a* limit and the ABox states the one the source reports. The distinction the old constraint reached for is real and is made in the manuscript rather than here: a non-detection establishes $[0,\mathrm{LOD}]$, and this record supports at best $[0,\mathrm{LOQ}]$ --- a weaker and different claim.

## Reading

The dominant finding is not a data-entry error. **2,306,365 rows (55.0\% of the river record) declare a result below the quantification limit and carry a positive number for it.** The substitution this paper is about has therefore already been performed, by the reporting authority, before anyone downloads the data. Where the flag survives, as here, the substitution is visible and can be undone. Where it does not, it cannot.
