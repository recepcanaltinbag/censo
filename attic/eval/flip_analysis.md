# Flip analysis: what changes when censoring is represented

Generated by `scripts/12_flip_analysis.py`. The same observations are assessed twice -- once as current practice would (a non-detect is a zero, two possible verdicts) and once with the censoring represented (a non-detect is $[0,\mathrm{LOD}]$, four possible verdicts).

## Headline

- observation--threshold pairs assessable at all: **8,400**
- verdicts that change: **7,246** (86.3%)
- of the **7,722** pairs standard practice reports as *compliant*, **7,236** (93.7%) are not supportable by the data
- of the **8,400** verdicts standard practice returns as determinate, **1,154** (13.7%) remain determinate once censoring is represented

### A conservative floor

The dominant cause above is a missing detection limit. A sceptic may argue that a laboratory reporting a zero presumably had a limit well below the standard, so those cases should be read as compliant. That presumption cannot be verified, and where it can be checked -- the neonicotinoids -- it is false. But granting it entirely still leaves **900 verdicts (10.7% of all assessable pairs)** that change because the method's quantification limit exceeds the standard, which no assumption about the laboratory can repair.

## Load bounds: what zero substitution hides

Substituting zero gives the smallest load consistent with the data; counting each non-detect at its own LOD gives the largest.

Evaluated at station **45**, the gauged station with the largest cumulative catchment area, so the figures are a river load rather than a sum over stations (which would double-count the same water).

| campaign | analytes | non-detects | lower bound (kg/d) | upper bound (kg/d) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 79 | 76 | 0.0294 | 0.795 | 27.0x |
| C2 | 79 | 72 | 0.487 | 1.24 | 2.6x |
| C3 | 79 | 77 | 0.0108 | 2.13 | 197.4x |
| C4 | 79 | 71 | 0.256 | 1.01 | 3.9x |

The lower bound is what zero substitution reports. The upper bound is equally consistent with the same measurements. Stating the first alone presents the most optimistic value compatible with the data as though it were the data.

## What could not be assessed at all

- observation--analyte pairs with no threshold in the regulation: **64,875**

These are not counted in the flip rate. Including them would inflate it; excluding them silently would hide how much of the survey the regulation does not reach.
