## Consequence

Every threshold used in this study must be transcribed from the regulation, not from the spreadsheet, and carry `cereg:transcriptionStatus cereg:VerifiedAgainstPrimarySource`. `scripts/validate_ontology.py` refuses to let unverified thresholds into an analysis.
